# Keep the calculator evidence-first and independent

The calculator will remain an independent, height-only tool for Chinese adult office users: every fit estimate must expose an evidence chain, trend estimates must disclose extrapolation, and source and formula auditing precedes interface expansion. The related sponsored episode is secondary content after the complete evidence chains and does not add sponsor branding or authority to the calculator; this preserves trust at the cost of reducing promotional prominence and requiring source-aware data maintenance.
