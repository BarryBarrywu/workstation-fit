# Model adjustments use rigid shells and telescoping segments

Visible robot and furniture shells retain their proportions as measurements change. Height variation is expressed by translating whole parts, rotating joints, or changing the exposed length of overlapping mechanical segments; non-uniform scaling of bodies, tabletops, seats, or screens is rejected because it visibly distorts the product and makes extreme-height poses unreliable. Adjustable assemblies must remain connected and collision-free across the full `145–205 cm` input range and permitted calibration offsets.
