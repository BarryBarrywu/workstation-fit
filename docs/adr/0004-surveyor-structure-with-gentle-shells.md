# Combine surveyor structure with gentle shells

The production robot will use Concept B's slender adult proportions and legible articulated structure, combined with Concept A's rounded head, softer shell transitions, and approachable caretaker character. The head will be a slightly flattened rounded capsule approximately 70% of the shoulder width: larger and gentler than Concept B, but clearly smaller than Concept A. The shoulders will be only about 10% wider than the hips, with a softly tapered, nearly straight torso rather than a heroic V-shaped armored chest. Each hand will be a rounded two-piece clamp that reads as one soft capsule when closed, without fingers or additional articulated joints. The entire design follows functional minimalism: only joints and details required to explain posture, adjustment, or identity remain visible. Exposed joints use a consistent dark graphite circular form showing only the pivot or telescoping sleeve, without gears, cables, or decorative pistons. Internal mechanisms remain visible only at the neck, elbows, waist, knees, and ankles; rounded shells cover the remaining structure to keep the model quiet and lightweight. All adjacent shells, collars, and sleeves must visibly overlap so the robot remains one connected machine; floating parts or cosmetic gaps fail review. Non-adjacent moving assemblies must retain visible pose clearance: hands and forearms may not intersect or visually fuse with the torso, pelvis, or thighs. The asymmetric amber head sensor and small amber chest light are the only identity details; antennas, serial markings, decals, visible fasteners, and brand logos are excluded. The approved production reference is `assets/concepts/robot-concept-final-turnaround.png`, containing front, side, rear, three-quarter, and seated validation views. Its slender silhouette and painted-metal surface character are acceptance criteria, not optional inspiration. Concept A alone was too stocky for clear ergonomic landmarks, while Concept C was too armored and heroic for a neutral workstation guide.
